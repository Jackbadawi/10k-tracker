Strength & safety

Keep hard leg workouts away from run days, cut leg volume if fatigue builds, and skip heavy legs the day before a run.

If the severe calf pain you reported earlier is still present, don't resume running — especially intervals or long runs — until it's been medically assessed and you can walk normally without pain. Stop if sharp pain returns.

Paces are provisional. If 6 km isn't comfortable yet, shorten the early runs and adjust paces to your current fitness.

Plan start

Pick the Monday of Week 1. Intervals fall on Wednesday and long runs on Sunday; race day is the Sunday of Week 5 (change it below if your race is another day).
